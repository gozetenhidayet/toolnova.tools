FAQ

How long should a ringtone be?

Most phones accept ringtones up to about 30–40 seconds; this tool defaults to 25 seconds, which works well for most devices.

How do I actually set it as my ringtone?

After downloading the MP3, you'll need to add it to your phone's ringtone settings — the exact steps depend on your phone (iPhone requires converting to a specific format via a sync tool; most Android phones can use the MP3 directly from Settings → Sound).

Can I pick which part of the song to use?

Yes, set the start time to whichever moment you want the ringtone to begin.
