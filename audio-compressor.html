FAQ

How much smaller will my file get?

Depends on the original — a WAV file re-encoded to 96 kbps MP3 often shrinks to a fraction of its original size; the tool shows you the exact before/after size.

Will lowering the bitrate hurt the sound quality?

Yes, somewhat — lower bitrates mean less audio detail. 128 kbps is a reasonable balance for most listening; go lower only if file size matters more than quality.

Does this work on already-compressed MP3s?

Yes, though compressing an already-compressed file further will reduce quality more noticeably than starting from a lossless source.
