How to use it

  1. Upload your audio recording.
  2. Adjust the sensitivity (how quiet counts as 'silence') and the minimum silence length to remove.
  3. Download the cleaned-up recording — see how much time was cut.

When it's useful

Useful for cleaning up a podcast or interview recording with long pauses, tightening a voice memo, or shortening a recording that has dead air between spoken sections.

FAQ

How does it decide what counts as silence?

It measures the loudness of short windows of audio and treats anything below your chosen sensitivity threshold as silence — background hiss and room tone can still register as 'quiet' depending on your setting.

Will it cut off words if I set it too aggressively?

It's possible with very sensitive settings on recordings with soft speech — start with the default and listen to the result, adjusting the sensitivity if it's too aggressive or not aggressive enough.

Can I control how short a silence has to be before it gets removed?

Yes, the minimum silence length setting means brief natural pauses (shorter than that) are left alone, while longer dead air is cut.
