How to use it

  1. Upload your audio file.
  2. Drag the slider to choose a speed from 0.5× to 2×.
  3. Download the re-rendered file at the new speed.

When it's useful

Useful for speeding up a long lecture or podcast recording to review it faster, or slowing down a recording to catch details in fast speech or complex audio.

FAQ

Does the pitch change when I change the speed?

Yes — like a record player playing faster or slower, pitch rises with speed increases and drops with speed decreases. This tool doesn't do pitch-preserving time-stretching.

What's the speed range?

0.5× (half speed) to 2× (double speed).

Does the file length change?

Yes — a file played at 2× speed becomes half as long; at 0.5× speed it becomes twice as long.
