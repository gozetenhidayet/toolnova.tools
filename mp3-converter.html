When it's useful

Useful when you've recorded a video and just want the audio track, when you have an old M4A/WAV file and need a more universally-compatible MP3, or when a video-editing app exported audio in a format your other tools don't accept.

FAQ

Does this download from YouTube or other sites?

No. This tool only converts files you already have on your device — it doesn't fetch or download content from any website.

What formats can I convert from?

Any video or audio format your browser can play, including MP4, MOV, WebM, M4A, WAV, OGG, and MP3 itself (useful for changing quality).

Is the audio quality good?

Yes — you choose the MP3 bitrate (128/192/320 kbps), with 192 kbps recommended for music and 128 kbps fine for speech.
