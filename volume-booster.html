FAQ

Will boosting the volume distort my audio?

With the clip protection option on, loud peaks are gently softened (using a smooth compression curve) instead of harshly clipping — this avoids the crackling distortion that happens when volume is pushed past the maximum.

Can I lower the volume too, not just raise it?

Yes, the slider goes from -80% (much quieter) to +200% (much louder).

Is this the same as normalizing audio?

Not exactly — this applies a flat percentage change across the whole file, rather than automatically adjusting to a target loudness level.
