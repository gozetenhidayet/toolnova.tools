How to use it

  1. Upload two or more audio files.
  2. Drag the files into the order you want them merged.
  3. Choose MP3 or WAV, then download the combined file.

When it's useful

Useful for combining multiple voice memo recordings into one, assembling a playlist-style single file, or joining split recordings back into one continuous file.

FAQ

Can I merge files of different formats?

Yes — you can mix MP3, WAV, M4A, and OGG files in a single merge; they're all decoded to the same format before combining.

Is there a limit on how many files I can merge?

No fixed limit — practical limits depend on your device's memory for very large batches.

Can I reorder the files after adding them?

Yes, drag any file's chip to a new position before merging.
